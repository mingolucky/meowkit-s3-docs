VU Meter turns nearby sound into a responsive analog display. Speak, play music, or make a sound—the needle follows its level in real time.

中文：VU Meter 将周围的声音呈现为实时变化的模拟指针。说话、播放音乐或轻敲桌面，指针都会随声音强弱移动。

## How it works

MeowKit captures sound through its built-in microphones and converts the signal into a smooth level reading. The scale runs from **−30 VU** to **+3 VU**.

中文：MeowKit 通过内置麦克风采集声音，并将信号转换为平滑的音量读数。表盘范围为 **−30 VU 至 +3 VU**。

VU Meter is designed for visualization. It is not a calibrated sound-pressure meter.

中文：VU Meter 用于直观观察声音变化，不属于经过校准的声压测量仪器。

## Use VU Meter

Open **Apps → VU Meter**. No setup is required.

For a clearer response, keep the microphone opening unobstructed and place MeowKit near the sound source.

中文：打开 **Apps → VU Meter** 即可使用，无需额外设置。

请勿遮挡麦克风开孔。将 MeowKit 靠近声源，可以获得更明显的指针变化。

Long-press **B** to exit.

中文：长按 **B** 退出应用。

## If the background is missing

VU Meter loads its dial image from:

`/vu_meter/vu_meter_bg.png`

Make sure the file is present on the SD card. If it cannot be loaded, the screen displays **BG PNG not found**.

中文：VU Meter 会从 SD 卡读取表盘图片：

`/vu_meter/vu_meter_bg.png`

如果文件缺失，屏幕会显示 **BG PNG not found**。
