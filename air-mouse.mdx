Turn MeowKit into a Bluetooth mouse. Rotate the device to move the pointer, use the buttons to click, and scroll with the joystick or touchscreen.

## Connect to your computer

1. Open **Air Mouse** on MeowKit.
2. Place MeowKit on a stable surface and keep it still during calibration.
3. Open your computer’s Bluetooth settings and select **MeowKit AirMouse**.
4. Wait for **CONNECTED** to appear on MeowKit.

When **KEEP STILL** changes to **Calibrate**, motion control is ready. Pick up MeowKit and gently rotate it to move the pointer.

## Click and scroll

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Press and release **A** | Left-click |
| Hold **A** while moving | Drag |
| Press and release **B** briefly | Right-click |
| Move the joystick up or down | Scroll |
| Swipe vertically in **Touch Scroll** | Scroll |
| Press and hold **B** | Exit Air Mouse |

Pointer movement pauses while you use the touch scroll panel, helping you scroll without moving away from your target.

## Adjust sensitivity

Choose **Low**, **Normal**, or **High** to change how far the pointer moves when you rotate MeowKit.

Move the joystick left or right to decrease or increase sensitivity. You can also tap **Speed** to cycle through the three levels.

Your selection is saved automatically.

## Calibrate motion

Calibration helps keep the pointer steady when MeowKit is not moving. It runs each time you open Air Mouse.

To recalibrate:

1. Place MeowKit on a stable surface.
2. Tap **Calibrate**.
3. Keep it still until **KEEP STILL** changes back to **Calibrate**.

If **TRY AGAIN** appears, leave MeowKit still and tap it to retry. Clicking and scrolling remain available while motion calibration is incomplete.

## Troubleshooting

### Paired, but not responding

Check the status on MeowKit:

- **WAITING** — waiting for a Bluetooth connection.
- **PAIRING** — connected, but the mouse input channel is not ready.
- **CONNECTED** — ready to send mouse input.
- **BLE ERROR** — Bluetooth could not start correctly. Exit Air Mouse and reopen it.

If the status stays at **PAIRING**, remove **MeowKit AirMouse** from your computer’s Bluetooth settings, then pair it again.

### Buttons work, but the pointer does not move

Check the calibration label. If it shows **KEEP STILL** or **TRY AGAIN**, complete calibration before using motion control.

### The pointer moves while MeowKit is still

Place MeowKit on a stable surface and tap **Calibrate**. Avoid touching or moving it until calibration finishes.

### IMU ERROR appears

The motion sensor is unavailable. Exit Air Mouse and reopen it. If the error remains, restart MeowKit.
