Air Mouse turns MeowKit into a Bluetooth mouse. Move the device to guide the pointer, use the physical buttons to click, and slide on the screen to scroll.

中文：Air Mouse 可将 MeowKit 变成蓝牙鼠标。移动设备控制指针，使用实体按键点击，在屏幕上滑动即可滚动页面。

## Pair with your device

1. Open **Apps → Air Mouse**.
2. Place MeowKit on a stable surface while the gyroscope calibrates.
3. Open Bluetooth settings on your computer or tablet.
4. Select **MeowKit AirMouse**.
5. Wait for the status to change from **WAITING** to **CONNECTED**.

中文：

1. 打开 **Apps → Air Mouse**。
2. 陀螺仪校准期间，将 MeowKit 平稳放置。
3. 打开电脑或平板的蓝牙设置。
4. 选择 **MeowKit AirMouse**。
5. 等待状态从 **WAITING** 变为 **CONNECTED**。

Keep MeowKit still during calibration. Moving it may cause the pointer to drift.

中文：校准期间请勿移动 MeowKit，否则指针可能产生漂移。

## Controls

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move pointer | Rotate or tilt MeowKit |
| Left click | Press **A** |
| Right click | Short-press **B** |
| Scroll | Swipe vertically inside **Touch Scroll** |
| Exit | Long-press **B** |

中文：

| 操作 | 控制方式 |
| --- | --- |
| 移动指针 | 转动或倾斜 MeowKit |
| 左键单击 | 按 **A** |
| 右键单击 | 短按 **B** |
| 页面滚动 | 在 **Touch Scroll** 区域上下滑动 |
| 退出应用 | 长按 **B** |

The globe shows the device’s tilt. **X** and **Y** show accumulated pointer movement.

中文：左侧球形界面用于显示设备倾斜状态，**X** 与 **Y** 显示累计指针位移。

## If Air Mouse does not connect

- Confirm that Bluetooth is enabled on the host device.
- Remove an old **MeowKit AirMouse** pairing, then pair again.
- Close and reopen Air Mouse to restart Bluetooth advertising.
- Keep MeowKit near the host during pairing.

中文：如果无法连接，请确认主机蓝牙已开启。必要时删除旧的 **MeowKit AirMouse** 配对记录，重新打开应用并再次配对。
